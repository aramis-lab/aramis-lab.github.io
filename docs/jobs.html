Job Offers

If you are interested in joining the team, do not hesitate to send us an email stating your field of interest and a CV. We may have other open positions than those advertised below.

You can directly contact one of the faculty members of the team, or the heads of the team for a general inquiry. Our team is pluridisciplinary and we welcome applicants with different profiles and expertise (computer science, electrical engineering, neuroimaging, neuroscience, medicine, etc.).

You can also check the ICM, Inria or CNRS websites to find our job offers.
