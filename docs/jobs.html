Job Offers

Our team is multidisciplinary and we welcome applicants with different profiles and expertise (computer science, electrical engineering, neuroimaging, neuroscience, medicine, etc.).

If you are interested in joining the team but do not see a position that matches your profile among those advertised below, we kindly ask you to complete our online application form using the link below, stating your field of interest and attaching your CV, rather than sending us an email. We may have open positions other than those advertised.

Application Form

Once we have received your information, it will be reviewed internally. If you do not receive a response within one month, this means that we unfortunately do not currently have a suitable position available for your profile.

You can also check the ICM, Inria or CNRS websites to find our job offers.
